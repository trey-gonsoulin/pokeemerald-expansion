# Balance: exp formula, story bosses and level curve

The fork's balance changes and the tools that model and apply them. The commands for `tools/area_archetypes` are in `CLAUDE.md`.

- Exp formula configs (#154, #189, #350), fork code in `src/exp_formula.c` (`include/exp_formula.h`), called from `ApplyExperienceMultipliers` in `src/battle_script_commands.c`, set in the fork's `include/config/battle.h` and listed in `BATTLE_CONFIG_DEFINITIONS` (`include/constants/config_changes.h`) for `WITH_CONFIG`. The owner first picked them in the curve explorer (#151); #350 (`findings.md` section 18) replaced the steep scaling with flat exp and softened the penalty:
  - `B_SCALED_EXP` is `GEN_6`, Gen 6's flat formula with no level scaling (#350). Scaled exp gives an under-levelled Pokémon much more exp per KO, so a player who skips trainers caught up to within 12-20% of one who fights them all; the two-player curve needs about 25 points between them. The 1.5x trainer exp stays off (`B_TRAINER_EXP_MULTIPLIER` `GEN_LATEST`).
  - `B_SCALED_EXP_STEEP` (#154): with scaled exp, the level weighting uses `gExperienceScalingFactorsSteep` (level^3.5) instead of level^2.5. On from #154 to #350, `FALSE` since: it does nothing under flat exp. Its code and its "Exp formula:" test stay; the test sets `B_SCALED_EXP` and the steep flag with `WITH_CONFIG`.
  - `B_EXP_OVERLEVEL_PENALTY`: above the fainted Pokémon's level, the exp follows a logistic curve in the percent of its level the getter is over it (#189, replacing #154's halving).
    - Its shape: full exp at the foe's level, falling slowly at first, fastest at `B_EXP_OVERLEVEL_CUTOFF` (30) percent over `B_EXP_OVERLEVEL_WIDTH` (8) percent (0: straight to the floor past the cutoff), then levelling out at `B_EXP_OVERLEVEL_FLOOR` (40) percent of the exp. The result is at least 1, and no exp stays none.
    - The values: #189's 15 / 4 / 10 (the owner's, `findings.md` section 8) became 30 / 8 / 40 in #350. `rebalance.py tune` swept cutoffs 20-40, widths 4, 6 and 8 and floors 20-40 against the solo grind budget (no budgeted solo wall over 50 KOs). Three passed, (30, 8, 40), (35, 6, 40) and (35, 8, 40), and the owner took the strictest. The penalty stays so the full clear doesn't snowball under flat exp.
    - `ApplyOverlevelPenalty` runs after every other multiplier, so it covers Exp. Share bench and catch exp too.
    - It's integer math: `sExpLogistic`, 4096 / (1 + e^t) in 16ths of t, interpolated in 256ths, then one u64 division. `tools/rebalance`'s `Overlevel.apply` computes the same, and both test suites pin the same cases.
    - The cutoff and width are at most 255 and the floor at most 100: the test override stores them in 8 bits (static assert).
    - `B_EXP_OVERLEVEL_MIN_LEVEL` (17): a foe below that level counts as that level for the cutoff and width, so they never shrink below a few levels (5.1 and 1.4 levels at 30 / 8). It came with #189's 15 / 4 / 10, where they were 2.55 and 0.68 levels: in percent of the foe's own level, a level-5 starter was 67% over a level-3 Zigzagoon and got the floor (2 exp, 39 Route 101 KOs to reach level 7); with the minimum it got 74% of the exp (6 KOs to level 7, 18 to level 8). Foes at 17 and above are unchanged. `Overlevel.min_level` models it and the explorer has a slider for it. The owner's playtest found the early game unplayable without it (no exp from the Route 101 wilds before the level-6 Route 103 rival).
    - #189's choice of 17: the highest minimum that left the Route 103 rival at 6 under #150's curve. `apply` then raised 42 teams by 1, all before Brawly: the Roxanne stage's trainers (the Petalburg Woods grunt among them) and Roxanne (ace 16), and in the Brawly stage the Rustboro rival (16 and 18), the Rusturf Tunnel grunt and seven route and gym trainers; Brawly and everything after him were unchanged. The Roxanne and Brawly stages' wild tables also rose by 1. At 18 and up the higher early bench carried through, and every later stage rose by 1 too (345 teams at 19). The raise re-flagged three default sets as one attack, hand-written by #159's rule: the Petalburg Woods and Rusturf Tunnel grunts' Poochyena 13 (Leer -> Tackle) and Billy's Zigzagoon 9 (Growl -> Tackle).
    - #350 kept 17. At 30 / 8 / 40 a level-5 starter reaches level 7 in 5 Route 101 KOs, and `Exp.test_early_exp_before_the_first_rival` in `tools/rebalance` fails over 8 (on the real Route 101 table, the least-exp foe every KO). Lower minimums down to 7 pass on today's Route 101, but the penalty was tuned at 17, and only 17 still passes if Route 101 falls back to its vanilla levels.
  - Covered by the "Exp formula:" tests in `test/battle/exp.c`. Its logistic test pins the configs, so retuning them fails there; the in-battle test sets its own (#189's 15 / 4 / 10 / 17). Tests that pin other parts of the formula turn the penalty off with `WITH_CONFIG`, since a level-20 mon gets about 40% of the exp from a level-10 foe: three upstream tests at the top of that file, the Lucky Egg test in `test/battle/move_effect/embargo.c`, `WITH_EXP_AWARD_CONFIGS` and `test/battle/hold_effect/exp_share.c`'s `WITH_EXP_SHARE_CONFIGS`. Add the same to a new test that measures exp across a level gap. Upstream's "Exp is scaled to player and opponent's levels" sets `B_SCALED_EXP` `GEN_LATEST` itself (#350: under `GEN_6` its `#if` had compiled it out).
  - `tools/rebalance` reads the configs (`game_scaling`, `Overlevel.from_configs`, and `explorer_defaults`' flat or scaled exp and 1.5x from `B_SCALED_EXP` and `B_TRAINER_EXP_MULTIPLIER` since #350; off in its vanilla regime), so `report.md`, `apply` and the explorer's defaults follow them.

## Wild tables

`tools/area_archetypes/README.md` documents the archetype rules and the restock options. The committed wild species are vanilla: `src/data/wild_encounters.json` is the restock with `tools/area_archetypes/restock_settings.json` (`{"ratio": 0}`, so only the level offsets apply), which `rebalance.py apply` and the pinning unittest pass as `--config` too. #28 had committed the default 0.5 restock (Gen 4-9 species such as Frillish on Route 110), and the owner turned it back for now; to restock again, edit that file and rerun `restock --write --config tools/area_archetypes/restock_settings.json`. `build` and `restock` read the committed vanilla snapshot, never the live `src/data/wild_encounters.json`, so `restock --write` reruns don't compound. Restock also applies the wild level offsets in `tools/area_archetypes/level_offsets.json` (#150), which `rebalance.py apply` writes.
